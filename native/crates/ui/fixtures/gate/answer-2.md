### Labels in three scripts

The sign above the second lamp now reads in each language you asked for:

- English: *Lamp two*
- Arabic, right to left: مصباح رقم اثنين يعمل بالرافعة
- Hebrew, right to left: מנורה שנייה עם ידית
- Japanese: 二番目のランプはレバーで点きます
- Chinese: 第二盏灯由拉杆控制

Each sign line holds at most fifteen characters, so the longer lines wrap onto a second sign.
