The transcript as a web area or a document, with an AT-SPI Collection patch (2026-11-17, adipc, Xvfb :97, Orca 51.1 from source).

Keys sent by the experiment after Orca starts: Shift+Tab (to the transcript), Home, Down x3, H (x2; x8 on the long fixture), K, L, T, Tab.
Orca ran its default script (the app is not a Gecko, Chromium or WebKit toolkit) and entered browse mode on the transcript.
Every H, K and L search went through Collection.GetMatches and returned at most one match: the virtual list has only the rows on screen in the tree.
On the long fixture (400 messages, a "Step N" heading every fourth message) H found "Step 395" each time and wrapped.
T found nothing (the adapter has no Table interface).
Down in browse mode read "blank": the arrows went to Orca's caret navigation, not to the app's message navigation.
The patch was dropped and the transcript stays a Region.
